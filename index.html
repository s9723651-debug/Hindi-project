Origin of Hindi

Tap each era to follow the language's journey.

📜 Script

Written in Devanagari, left to right, with a horizontal line (शिरोरेखा) on top of letters.

🏛️ Official status

Hindi in Devanagari was adopted as an official language of India's Union on 14 September 1949.

🌏 Spread

Spoken across India and by diaspora in Fiji, Mauritius, Nepal, Trinidad, the Gulf, the UK, USA and beyond.

🔤 Words you know

Shampoo, jungle, bungalow, pyjamas, avatar, karma, yoga and thug all entered English from Hindi.
